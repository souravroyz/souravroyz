About Me

From Campus
To Core Industry

I'm Sourav Roy — a B.Tech ECE graduate working as an Industrial Automation Engineer. I know exactly how hard it is to break into core electronics and electrical roles after graduation, because I've lived it.

While most career channels push IT/software as the only path for ECE students, I'm here to show you the real opportunities in automation, control systems, SCADA, and core electronics — roles that actually use what you studied.

Through souravroy.co and my YouTube channel, I share roadmaps, resources, certifications, and industry realities to help you land the job that matches your degree.
